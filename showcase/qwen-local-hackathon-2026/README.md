# Qwen Local Hackathon 2026

A free online hackathon organized by **Qwen Pakistan**. Build something useful with a small Qwen model. Do at least one of these: fine-tune it, quantize it, or run it locally.

- Kickoff: Monday 23 November 2026, on Microsoft Teams. Follow the event page for meeting details.
- Build week: 23 to 29 November 2026.
- Submissions close: **Saturday 28 November 2026, 23:59 PKT (18:59 UTC)**.
- Results: Sunday 29 November 2026.

[Event page](https://luma.com/zymwkh7c) · [Qwen Pakistan community on WhatsApp](https://chat.whatsapp.com/KJ7NTEU86UKEdphUQI2vOP)

## The challenge

Pick a task you can explain and solve it with a small Qwen model. Fine-tuning is optional: running a Qwen model locally in a useful app counts. If you fine-tune, publish the model and real held-out before/after examples. Every entry needs a clear demo video and a write-up another person can follow. Use data you have permission to use and follow the base model's license.

This is community-organized material, not an official Alibaba or Qwen team repository.

## Judging

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Usefulness | 25% | A real problem solved for someone |
| Proof it works | 25% | Fine-tunes: base-versus-tuned results on held-out examples. Other entries: real test inputs and outputs |
| Runs locally and efficiently | 25% | Runs on a normal laptop or free GPU, hardware stated |
| Demo and write-up | 25% | Clear video and instructions another person can follow |

Winners need a ModelScope account to receive rewards.

## Submit

Follow [the submission guide](../README.md#submit-your-model), using `showcase/qwen-local-hackathon-2026/your-slug/`. Open one PR containing only your entry folder. A demo video is required. Post it publicly on LinkedIn or X, tag M Junaid and Qwen Pakistan, use #QwenLocalHackathon, and put the post link in `demo_video`. A public model link is required only if you fine-tuned.

After opening the PR, fill the submission form shared in the WhatsApp group and on the event page. An entry needs both the PR and the form. Do not commit model weights or files over 5 MB.

Eligibility uses the PR's creation time. The closing minute is inclusive: PRs at or after 19:00:00 UTC are late. Late entries are welcome, receive a `late` label and are not judged. You can move them to `community/` instead. No stars or follows are required.
